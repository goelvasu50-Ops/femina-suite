FEMINA DESK v7.1 — HOW TO UPLOAD / UPDATE
==========================================

Files inside this ZIP:
  index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, _headers

1) GITHUB PAGES (github.com/goelvasu50-ops/femina-suite):
   - Unzip this file on your phone.
   - Open your repo in Chrome (turn on Desktop site).
   - Tap "Add file" -> "Upload files" (or "uploading an existing file").
   - Select: index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png
   - Tap "Commit changes". Wait 1-2 mins and refresh your github.io link!

2) CLOUDFLARE WORKERS (feminasuite.goelvasu50.workers.dev):
   - Open dash.cloudflare.com -> Workers & Pages -> feminasuite -> Deployments.
   - Upload all 6 app files together -> Deploy.

3) NETLIFY:
   - Open app.netlify.com -> your site -> Deploys -> drop this ZIP directly.
